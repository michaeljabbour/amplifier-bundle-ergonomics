# Cognitive accessibility

Design for variation in attention, memory, executive function, language, literacy, learning, perception, stress, fatigue, and time processing.

## Check

- one clear purpose and next action at each decision point
- recognition instead of unaided recall
- stable location, labeling, and interaction patterns
- plain language with necessary technical precision
- chunking, summaries, examples, and progressive disclosure
- control over timing, motion, interruptions, and sensory density
- visible progress, state, consequences, and completion
- error prevention without punitive dead ends
- preserved work and concrete recovery instructions
- alternatives to memory-dependent or puzzle-like authentication
- support for reorientation after interruption

Avoid diagnosing users or treating one cognitive profile as universal. Test important tasks with people whose access needs match the product context.
