# Macroergonomics and sociotechnical fit

Evaluate the whole work system: people, roles, technology, process, incentives, environment, and organizational structure.

## Lenses

- role clarity and authority
- division of work across humans, agents, and services
- handoffs, escalation, and exception ownership
- communication burden and coordination latency
- local optimization that creates downstream work
- incentives that encourage workarounds or under-reporting
- shift changes, interrupted work, and institutional memory
- training, staffing, support, procurement, and maintenance
- incident response, auditability, and redress
- deployment, monitoring, recalibration, and retirement

## Output

Map actors, responsibilities, information flows, decisions, handoffs, failure containment, and feedback loops. Name where the interface is not the true intervention point.
